Key Trade-offs

  • Latency vs Accuracy — smaller models vs smarter agents
  • Cost vs Autonomy — more reasoning & tools = higher cost
  • Statefulness vs Scalability — persistent memory vs horizontal scale
  • Single vs Multi-Agent — simplicity vs collaboration power
  • Inference Optimization vs Quality — quantization / batching / speculative decoding cut cost, risk quality
