# Stage 09 — Prototype Summary

**Purpose:** record what was prototyped, its status, and what it actually
validated — or state clearly that no prototype was built.

**Inputs:** `08-visual-strategy.md`; the Claude Design draft; any code/clickable
prototype.

**Do:**
1. **Status** — Generated / Partial / Not run. If not run, say why and what that
   leaves unvalidated.
2. **What it is** — artboards vs clickable vs coded; where it lives (`prototype/`).
3. **What it validated** — which flows / states hold up; what it exposed (dead
   ends, missing states, confusing IA).
4. **What it did not cover** — flows still on paper only.
5. **Open design questions** → §15.

**Produce — `09-prototype-summary.md`:** Status · What it is · Validated · Not
covered · Open questions.

**Gate:** honest status. "Not run" is acceptable; pretending otherwise is not.
