## The through-line

Against **every ops competitor**: they reach the same answer only with a separate
security product, a separate budget, and (usually) a separate team — our ICP has
none of those. Against **every security competitor**: they don't touch
observability, and most assume a SOC or a CISO our ICP doesn't have.
