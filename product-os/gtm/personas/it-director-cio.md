# Persona: IT Director / Head of Infrastructure / CIO

## Overview

**Titles:** IT Director, Head of Infrastructure, Head of IT, VP IT, CIO (at the
smaller end of the range).
**Seniority:** Director / VP / C-suite.
**Decision role:** **Economic buyer.** Champion too, in orgs under ~300.
**Found at:** 100–1,000-employee companies with an IT org of 20–80, no SOC, one
team owning uptime and security.

## What they care about

- **Primary metric:** availability / uptime, staying within budget, passing
  audits — and increasingly "can we do more without more headcount."
- **Biggest problem right now:** two toolchains and two bills for what feels like
  one job; the team is stretched; audit season is a scramble; every incident is a
  fire drill that pulls in people who should be building.
- **Good week:** no Sev1, audit evidence produced without a scramble, a renewal
  negotiated down.
- **Bad week:** a prolonged incident where nobody could say fast whether it was
  an attack; a surprise renewal quote; an auditor finding.

## How they buy

- **Involvement:** initiates and approves. Controls the budget both tools come
  out of.
- **Discovery:** peer references (other IT Directors at similar-size companies),
  analyst validation (Gartner), vendor content that respects their time.
- **Evaluation style:** wants a POC their own team can run; skeptical of "AI
  magic"; cares about operational simplicity and total cost.
- **Common objections:**
  - "We already have Datadog." → renewal timing + single-platform TCO; the
    security spend you're about to add anyway.
  - "Is the AI going to hallucinate root causes?" → the linked-evidence
    timeline — you can check its work.
  - "We're too small for a platform like this." → that's the design point; it's
    built for a team without a SOC.

## How to reach them

- **Best channel:** warm intro > email > LinkedIn. Regional CIO / IT-leader
  roundtables.
- **What gets attention:** budget math, a peer's name, audit pain, "one team,
  two bills."
- **What gets ignored:** feature lists, "autonomous operations" as an opener,
  deep technical claims.

## Message framework

- **Value prop:** "Replace a monitoring bill and a security bill with one
  platform your existing team can run."
- **Proof points:** single-platform TCO vs. APM + emerging security spend; audit
  evidence without a scramble; a similar-size reference (anonymised by type until
  a logo exists).

## Sample outreach hook

> Renewal quotes on [incumbent] are up sharply this year, and the teams feeling
> it worst are the ones with no separate security budget to spread the cost
> across. Before you re-sign, worth seeing what one platform for uptime *and*
> security — run by the team you already have — looks like.
