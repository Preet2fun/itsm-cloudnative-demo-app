## Rollup through week 3

- Open rate **64%** · reply rate **12.0%** · positive reply **7.4%** · meeting
  rate **3.7%** · 2 POCs started (incl. **Meridian Freight** — replied to touch 1,
  meeting booked day 5, POC scoped to their late-Oct renewal).
- **Above target** on open, reply, positive reply. **Below** the 5% meeting-rate
  target (3.7%) — replies are strong but not all converting to a call.

## Patterns

- **Reply by touch:** touch 1 → 4 replies · touch 3 (TCO breakdown) → **6
  replies** · touch 4 (POC offer) → 3. The TCO breakdown is the workhorse —
  confirms the hypothesis.
- **Renewal proximity:** accounts <8 weeks from renewal replied at **18%**;
  8–13 weeks at **7%**. Proximity matters more than expected.
- One reply: *"good timing, but we already re-signed last month."* → that account
  was mis-tagged on renewal date. Enrichment gap logged.

## Decision (week 3): **Continue — with one iteration**

- Reply ≥ target and meetings booking → **Continue** per
  [`../../workflows/campaign-build.md`](../../workflows/campaign-build.md).
- **Iterate:** meeting rate is under target — the ask in touch 4 is a
  20-minute call; test a lower-friction ask (send the host-count TCO first, offer
  the call after). Rewrite touch 4 for the week-4 cohort.
- **Tighten:** only enrol accounts with a renewal date verified <13 weeks out
  (the mis-tag above; the 8–13-week cohort under-performs anyway).
