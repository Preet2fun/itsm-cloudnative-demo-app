# Lens 1 — Signal Scan

**Purpose:** size a pain and its trajectory before discovery spends time on it.

**Question:** Is this pain real, growing, and whose is it?

**Inputs:** the topic (a theme, a feature area, or "open scan"); a window
(default: last 30 days vs the prior 30); the data source; `../../context-hub/icp.md`
for segments; `../../context-hub/competitive-landscape.md` for competitor names.

**Do:**
1. **Volume** — distinct accounts raising it, this window vs the baseline.
2. **Trend** — rising / flat / fading; call out a spike and when it started.
3. **Severity** — sentiment split; count escalation language ("blocker",
   "considering alternatives", "churn").
4. **Whose** — distribution across tier / segment; is it concentrated?
5. **Contrast** — competitor mentions tied to the topic (fixed set only): what
   do customers say the alternative does better?
6. **Map** — to a `../../knowledge-hub/` feature, or flag as no current coverage.

**Produce — a briefing:** volume + trend numbers · severity read · segment
distribution · competitor-contrast notes · 3–5 quotes · Limitations.

**Feeds:** `ai-discovery/` stages 01–03; scores `opportunity-scorecard.md`
Magnitude (accounts), Frequency (recurrence over time), Severity (escalation +
sentiment), Competition + Contrast (competitor mentions). Also feeds
`gtm/signal-library.md` — the *consolidation / alert-fatigue post* Tier-2 signal
routes through this lens (don't build a second detector). Each figure tagged
`[Feedback: …]`.
